# About

This Government Polytechnic Kashipur PYQs Archive website was created for Polytechnic Students.

This site is not affiliated with Government Polytechnic Kashipur or any of its
campuses. This site is created for the students of Government Polytechnic Kashipur to access previous year
question papers easily.

## Why this website?

If you are using this site, then you know why.

Government Polytechnic Kashipur PYQs is a student-driven initiative to collect and preserve previous year question papers for Diploma Engineering and D.Pharma students.

Students can access semester-wise and year-wise PYQs for exam preparation and academic reference.
